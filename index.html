Model performance

AlgorithmExtreme gradient boosting (XGBoost), logistic objective, 310 trees
Stopping rule5-fold cross-validation, early stopping after 50 rounds without improvement
Hyperparameters800 rounds maximum, learning rate 0.03, depth 3, row and column subsample 0.8, minimum child weight 20, L2 penalty 2
InputsAge, sex, SOFA score, anion gap, bicarbonate, lactate, creatinine, all from the first 12 h
Derived termssev_z = (SOFA − 4.0638) / 2.243; missing lactate set to 2.4 mmol/L and missing creatinine to 1.45 mg/dL, each with an indicator
DevelopmentMIMIC-IV, 10,304 patients, 6,567 events
External validationeICU-CRD, SICdb, NWICU
BandsTertiles of predicted probability, 0.53 and 0.77; observed failure to recover 37.5%, 64.6% and 89.0%
SoftwareR 4.6.1, Python 3.13, XGBoost; predictions exact to 1×10-6 against the analysis object
CohortnEventsAUC (95% CI)Brier

Discrimination is adequate but calibration is not perfect: slope 1.12 in the development cohort, 0.63, 0.97 and 0.76 in the three external cohorts. Before local use the model should be recalibrated to the receiving centre. The 0.53 and 0.77 cut-points are tertiles of the predicted probability in the development cohort, at which observed failure to recover was 37.5%, 64.6% and 89.0%.

Cohort definitions, predictor encoding, verification steps and limitations: How the model was built.

Research prototype. Trained on retrospective multi-centre ICU data, not prospectively validated, and not a substitute for clinical judgement. Predictors are restricted to the first 12 h; the 24–48 h bicarbonate that defines the outcome is not used. Model: XGBoost, 310 trees, nine predictors. Reproduction of the published model object; predictions are exact to 1×10-6.
